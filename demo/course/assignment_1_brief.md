# Assignment 1: Research Proposal (30%)

## Task
Write a research proposal of 1,500 words (±10%) for a small study related to your own field or workplace.

## Your proposal must include
1. A clear research question and two or three objectives
2. A short justification: why this question matters
3. The research method you will use (qualitative, quantitative or mixed) and why
4. Your sampling approach and how you will collect data
5. Ethical considerations, including how you will get informed consent
6. A timeline for the study
7. A reference list with at least 8 academic sources

## Marking criteria
| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Research question and objectives | 20% |
| Choice and justification of method | 30% |
| Ethics | 20% |
| Use of literature | 20% |
| Structure, clarity and referencing | 10% |

Submit as a single Word or PDF file through the Assignment 1 link in the virtual classroom.
