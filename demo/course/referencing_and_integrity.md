# Referencing and Academic Integrity

## Referencing style
All written work must use **Harvard referencing**. Cite sources in the text with the author's surname and year, for example (Smith, 2021), and list every source in a reference list at the end, in alphabetical order by surname.

Direct quotations must include a page number, for example (Smith, 2021, p. 14).

## Similarity check
Every assignment is checked automatically for similarity with published sources and other students' work. A similarity score above 25%, excluding the reference list, is reviewed by the module leader.

## Use of AI tools
Students may use AI tools to brainstorm ideas, check grammar and improve the clarity of their own writing. Students may **not** submit text generated by an AI tool as their own work. Any use of AI tools must be declared in a short statement at the end of the assignment.

Undeclared use of AI-generated text is treated as academic misconduct.

## Academic misconduct
Plagiarism, collusion and undeclared AI-generated text can lead to a mark of zero for the assignment and, for repeated cases, to a formal disciplinary hearing.
