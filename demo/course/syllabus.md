# RM501 Research Methods: Course Syllabus

Northbridge Online University (fictional) · Master's programme · 8 weeks · 10 ECTS

## Module leader
Dr. Elena Varga. Office hours are held online every Wednesday, 18:00–19:00 (UTC+2), in the virtual classroom.

## Assessment
| Component | Weight | Due |
|---|---|---|
| Discussion forum participation (weeks 1–6) | 10% | Weekly, Sunday 23:59 |
| Assignment 1: Research proposal (1,500 words) | 30% | End of week 4 |
| Assignment 2: Literature review (3,000 words) | 60% | End of week 8 |

The pass mark for the module is 50%. Each assignment must score at least 40% to pass the module.

## Weekly topics
1. What is research? Research questions and objectives
2. Qualitative, quantitative and mixed methods
3. Sampling and data collection
4. Research ethics and consent
5. Surveys and questionnaire design
6. Interviews and thematic analysis
7. Writing a literature review
8. Presenting and defending your findings
