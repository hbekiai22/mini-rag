# Late Submissions and Extensions

## Late penalty
Assignments submitted after the deadline lose 5 percentage points per calendar day, for up to 7 days. Work submitted more than 7 days late receives a mark of zero.

Example: an assignment marked 68% that is submitted 2 days late receives 58%.

## Extensions
Students may request an extension of up to 10 days for illness, bereavement or other serious circumstances. Requests must be made through the Student Support form **before** the deadline and include supporting evidence, such as a medical certificate.

Workload, technical problems on the day of the deadline, or forgetting the deadline are not accepted as reasons for an extension.

## Resubmission
A student who fails an assignment may resubmit it once. Resubmitted work is capped at 50%.
