# Warranty — Orbit Bikes

Every Orbit frame is covered by a lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects for the original owner. The electric drive system (motor, controller, and display) is covered for 3 years. Batteries are covered for 2 years or 800 full charge cycles, whichever comes first; a battery is considered defective if it holds less than 70% of its original capacity within that period.

The warranty does not cover normal wear parts: tyres, brake pads, chains, cassettes, and grips. Damage from crashes, racing, commercial delivery use, or unauthorised modifications (including speed-limit "tuning") voids the warranty on affected parts.

To make a claim, contact support with your frame serial number, which is stamped under the bottom bracket, and a photo or video of the problem. Approved claims are repaired at a partner shop or shipped a replacement part free of charge.
