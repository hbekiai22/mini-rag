# Returns Policy — Orbit Bikes

Orbit Bikes accepts returns of unused bikes within 30 days of delivery. The bike must be in its original packaging with all accessories, manuals, and the battery key included. To start a return, open the Orbit app, go to Orders, and choose "Start a return". You will receive a prepaid shipping label by email within one business day.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days after the warehouse inspects the bike. A restocking fee of 10% applies to bikes that show signs of riding, up to a maximum of 50 kilometres on the odometer. Bikes with more than 50 kilometres cannot be returned and must go through the warranty process instead.

Accessories such as helmets, locks, and panniers can be returned within 60 days if unused. Gift cards and custom-painted frames are final sale.
