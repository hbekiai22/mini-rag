# Bike Models — Orbit Bikes

## Orbit City
A step-through commuter bike. 250 W hub motor, 500 Wh battery, range of 60–90 km. Weight 21 kg. Assisted top speed 25 km/h. Comes with integrated lights, mudguards, and a rear rack rated for 25 kg. Price: €1,890.

## Orbit Trail
A hardtail e-mountain bike. 85 Nm mid-drive motor, 630 Wh battery, range of 50–110 km depending on terrain. Weight 23.5 kg. 120 mm front suspension and 29-inch wheels. Price: €3,250.

## Orbit Cargo
A longtail cargo bike that carries two children or up to 80 kg of cargo on the rear deck. 90 Nm mid-drive motor and dual-battery support (2 × 500 Wh) for a range of up to 160 km. Weight 32 kg. Price: €4,400.

All models charge from empty to full in about 4.5 hours with the standard 4 A charger. A 6 A fast charger is sold separately and cuts this to about 3 hours.
