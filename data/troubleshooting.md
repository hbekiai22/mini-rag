# Troubleshooting — Orbit Bikes

## The display shows error E07
E07 means the motor is not receiving signal from the speed sensor. Check that the spoke magnet on the rear wheel is aligned with the sensor on the chainstay and is no more than 5 mm away from it. Then restart the bike by holding the power button for 10 seconds.

## The battery won't charge
First confirm the charger light turns red when plugged into the battery. If it stays green, the battery may be in deep-sleep mode after being stored empty; leave it on the charger for 12 hours to wake it. Store batteries at 40–60% charge if you won't ride for more than a month.

## Pedal assist cuts out over bumps
This usually means a loose battery connection. Remove the battery, clean the contacts with a dry cloth, and reinsert it until you hear a click. Lock it with the battery key.

## Updating firmware
Firmware updates are installed through the Orbit app over Bluetooth. Keep the bike within 2 metres of your phone and above 30% charge during the update, which takes about 10 minutes.
