# Shipping — Orbit Bikes

Orbit Bikes ships to all EU countries, the United Kingdom, Norway, and Switzerland. Standard delivery is free and takes 3–5 business days. Express delivery takes 1–2 business days and costs €49. Orders placed before 14:00 CET on a business day leave the warehouse the same day.

Bikes arrive 95% assembled. You only need to attach the handlebar and pedals; a multi-tool is included in the box. For €79 you can choose "Ready to ride" delivery, where a technician assembles and adjusts the bike at your door.

Orders to the United Kingdom, Norway, and Switzerland may be charged import duties on delivery, which are the customer's responsibility.
